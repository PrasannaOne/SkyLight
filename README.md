## 🌌 About SkyLight

**SkyLight** is an infinite, meditative web-based celestial drawing sandbox. Designed for pure relaxation and tactile joy, it features a buttery-smooth infinite panning and zooming canvas, warm felt-harp audio feedback, cozy background horizons, and persistent state saving.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Infinite Procedural Cosmos**: Explore and draw across an endless night sky generated organically using Poisson disk starfields.
* **Pure Star Weaving**: Touch any star to begin, and swipe smoothly from star to star. Up to 5 branch connections per star give you total creative freedom.
* **Warm Acoustic Harp Audio**: Every connection sounds a low-pass filtered, slow acoustic harp note mapped to pentatonic scales.
* **Persistent Auto-Save**: Your constellations, camera positions, and settings are saved automatically via `localStorage` so your art stays intact across refreshes.
* **Lively Background Atmosphere**: Subtle, slow-gliding deep-space meteors and shooting stars breathe life into the infinite space.
* **Cozy Color Themes**: Switch instantly between **White**, **Blue**, **Pink**, and **Gold** ambient palettes.
* **Silent 10-Step Undo**: Easily step backward through your last 10 connections.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open the live site: [https://prasannaone.github.io/SkyLight/](https://prasannaone.github.io/SkyLight/)
2. **Touch/Click** any star to anchor your thread.
3. **Glide** to nearby stars to weave your custom constellation.
4. **Drag on empty space** or use **two fingers** to pan and zoom across the universe.
